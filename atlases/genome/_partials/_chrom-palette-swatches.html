Chromosome palette — --ga-chrom-1…--ga-chrom-13 + --ga-chrom-fused

Categorical hue per chromosome. Slots 1–11 step by 30° around the colour wheel; slots 12–13 are 15°-step infills for karyotypes with >11 chroms; the fused token is a half-step (135°) reserved for fused/transition blocks (used on page_synteny V3). Paint an SVG <rect> or <polygon> with the matching ga-chrom-N utility class.
