Headline assembly QC for the F₁ hybrid (C. gariepinus × C. macrocephalus) haplotype-resolved genome. One banner at the top summarizes global numbers; a per-chromosome table breaks them down by haplotype (Gar: 28 chroms, Mac: 27 chroms) with a T2T completeness chip per chromosome row.
This is the page reviewers will land on first when checking the assembly paper's claims — the numbers here back every assembly-quality sentence in the methods + results.
