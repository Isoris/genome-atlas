What this page shows
phase B · numbers from fixture
Headline assembly QC for the F₁ hybrid (C. gariepinus × C. macrocephalus) haplotype-resolved genome. One banner at the top summarizes global numbers; a per-haplotype tile pair breaks them down by species; a per-chromosome table per haplotype carries QV, BUSCO count, centromere status, telomere chips, and a T2T verdict. The BUSCO and Merqury breakouts below the per-chrom table are the forensic level — they let reviewers see why a given haplotype lands at QV 45 / 97% complete.
The values displayed here come from the fixture at fixtures/assembly_qc/assembly_stats.json (Q45+ Merqury, 97% BUSCO complete, ~33 Mb scaffold N50) — realistic for the assembly paper's QC target. When data/assembly/assembly_stats.json lands from the cluster the renderer swaps it in unchanged.

QV bands. Q40+ = manuscript-ready (≤ 1 error per 10 kb). Q35–39.9 = good but not VGP-spec. Q30–34.9 = acceptable for variant calling, flagged in review. k-mer completeness is the fraction of solid truth-set k-mers that made it into the assembly — both haplotypes recover >97%, and the union recovers >99.8%. The 0.14% gap in the union is het-mosaicism the parents didn't actually carry. Switch error is the fraction of trio-aware mismatches (where a paternal k-mer appears on a maternal-tagged scaffold or vice versa) — both haplotypes land < 0.025%.
