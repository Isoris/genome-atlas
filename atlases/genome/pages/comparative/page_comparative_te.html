Important caveat: only 64 / 377 Gar breakpoints are base-precise; 83 % sit on round 50 kb multiples (binned). Enrichment in ±50 kb measures neighbourhood density, not what sits ON the junction. The DNA-transposon "dominance" (117 / 202) is a raw count, not enrichment. Whether it's real enrichment depends on the genome-wide background — see the TE12 upgrade (breakpoint_TEclass_enrichment_<F>.tsv): Fisher + fold-enrichment with Bonferroni against intact-TE genome-wide background. fold > 1.5 and sig → real enrichment; fold ~ 1 → abundance only.

Intact-TE BED reference
2 files · 9 304 + ~6 500 elements
The two large EDTA-intact BED files (the per-element track for each haplotype) are not inlined in the consolidated envelope. They feed the breakpoint cross-analysis (TE12) and the page_chromosome_overview density tracks. Format mirrors EDTA's intact GFF3 but in BED form — 1-based half-open chrom/start/end + name + classification + score. 
# fClaHyb_{Gar,Mac}_LG.fa.mod.EDTA.intact.bed   (EDTA v2.2 intact track)
#
# Tab-separated. 12 columns:
#   col 1   chrom            (C_gar_LGNN | C_mac_LGNN)
#   col 2   start_bp_0based  (BED half-open)
#   col 3   end_bp_0based
#   col 4   element_id       (TE_NNNNNNN)
#   col 5   classification   (DNA/DTA | LTR/Gypsy | DNA/Helitron | ...)
#   col 6   call_method      (structural | homology)
#   col 7   identity         (0..1; age proxy — higher = younger)
#   col 8-10 reserved        (typically '.')
#   col 11  attributes       (TSD=...; TIR=...; motif=...; tsd=...)
#   col 12  superfamily      (DNA | LTR | LINE | ...)
#   col 13  family           (e.g. TIR/hAT, LTR/Gypsy)
