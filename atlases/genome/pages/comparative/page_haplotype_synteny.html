▰ Haplotype synteny — fClaHyb_Gar ↔ fClaHyb_Mac
Stage: comparative · phase D · driven by haplotype_synteny_blocks layer (wfmash whole-genome alignment, focal pair)
What this page shows
phase D · focal Gar ↔ Mac only
The whole-genome alignment-based synteny relationship between the two F₁ haplotypes (fClaHyb_Gar = C. gariepinus, 28 chroms; fClaHyb_Mac = C. macrocephalus, 27 chroms). Five views: ribbon panels per Gar chrom, a focal Oxford dotplot, the headline LG28↔(LG06+LG01) fusion/fission event panel (the n=28 vs n=27 karyotype difference at sequence level), strand-orientation summary, and the schema contract.
Scope: focal pair only. The cross-species panel (17 catfish genomes, all-vs-all wfmash, ALG collinearity, mashmap triage) lives in a separate cross-species atlas. page_comparative_te in this atlas covers the cross-species TE landscape; this page covers the within-focal-pair structural relationship.

Source: fixtures/haplotype_synteny/synteny_blocks.json (schema v2 from STEP_CS01_extract_breakpoints.py); renderer helpers live alongside in fixtures/haplotype_synteny/synteny_figures/ (the synteny-figures Python package that emits PDF/PNG figures off the same JSON for the manuscript).

View 1 — synteny ribbons (per Gar chrom)
42 blocks · 29 inverted · 13 same-strand
One panel per Gar chrom with at least one aligned block. Top bar = Gar chrom (coral); bottom bar(s) = Mac partner chrom(s) (teal). Ribbons connect aligned blocks; blue = same strand, coral = inverted strand. Width of each bar = chrom length; ribbon endpoints = block start / end in each genome.

LG23 → LG01 16 blocks · 17.0 Mb · 16 inverted
LG27 → LG01 16 blocks · 14.9 Mb · 11 inverted
LG28 → LG01, LG06 8 blocks · 7.3 Mb · 0 inverted
The Gar haplotype's 4 chroms with synteny-block calls in this fixture (LG15, LG23, LG27, LG28) cover the manuscript's headline events. LG28 is the standout — it splits across both Mac LG06 and Mac LG01 (see V3 below), which is the sequence-level signature of the n=28 vs n=27 karyotype difference between the two species.
View 2 — focal Oxford dotplot
4 × 2 cells · dot ∝ block bp · colour by dominant strand
Each cell = (Gar chrom × Mac chrom). Dot radius scales with total aligned bp; colour reflects the dominant strand (blue = collinear, coral red = inverted, amber = mixed). Number inside each dot = block count for that pair. Empty cells = no aligned blocks ≥ 50 kb in this fixture. LG28 is the only Gar chrom landing dots in two Mac columns (LG06 + LG01) — the fusion/fission signature.
Single-cell-dominant pairs (LG15→LG06, LG23→LG01, LG27→LG01) read as 1:1 conserved chromosome pairs in the focal alignment. LG28 → two columns reads as the fission/fusion event.
View 3 — Headline event: LG28 ↔ (LG06 + LG01)
n=28 vs n=27 karyotype difference at sequence level
Gar LG28 splits its aligned content across two Mac chroms: LG06 (the bulk, 6.6 Mb in 6 blocks) and LG01 (the lesser arm, 0.8 Mb in 2 blocks). This is the sequence-level signature of the karyotype difference — Gar carries 28 chromosomes, Mac carries 27, and the difference resolves to one ancestral chromosome that either fused in the Mac lineage or fissioned in the Gar lineage.
Polarisation (which state is ancestral) needs an outgroup — per the Chapter 5 mashmap triage, against N. graeffei Mac LG06 also splits onto Gar (LG03 + LG28), suggesting the LG03/LG06 junction region is labile across this clade. The full polarisation analysis lives in the cross-species atlas; this page just surfaces the focal-pair signature.
View 4 — Strand orientation (per BP0 pre-pass)
16 FLIP / 12 KEEP · assembly-convention only
From MODULE_BP0_orient/STEP_BP0a_detect_orientation.py: per Gar chrom, the bp-weighted minus-strand fraction against Mac. A high minus-fraction (≥ 0.90) flags a chromosome that is almost entirely convention-flipped — a strand-mirroring artefact of how each assembly fixed its scaffolds, NOT a biological inversion. 16 of 28 Gar chroms are FLIP (convention-mirrored); 12 are KEEP; LG27 (~77% reverse) and LG04 (~87%) are mixed — they read mostly-reverse but carry a real internal inversion on an otherwise-flipped chromosome (deliberately KEEP so the real inversion survives in the breakpoint caller).
